On contract telecom and cabling jobs the client usually isn't on site. The only proof of a good install is what you send back. Most service agreements expect deliverables **within hours** of finishing — here's the package I prepare every time.

## 1. Check in, check out

Before touching anything, report to the contact named on the work order. Do the same before leaving. It creates a timestamped record of when you were on site and avoids "nobody saw the tech" disputes.

## 2. Cable test results

Export the certifier or tester report for **every** run:

- Cable ID that matches the label on both ends
- Pass/fail with length, wiremap and the standard tested against (e.g. Cat 6 permanent link)
- Re-test anything marginal before you leave — a return trip costs more than ten minutes now

## 3. Photos that tell the story

| Photo | Why |
|---|---|
| Rack / patch panel before | Proves the starting condition |
| Rack / patch panel after | Shows dressing, labeling and cable management |
| Each drop location | Confirms the outlet, label and faceplate |
| Equipment serial labels | Matches devices to the asset list |
| Any damage or obstacle found | Protects you when something was already broken |

Name files by site and cable ID so the reviewer can match them without asking.

## 4. Sign-off and punch list

- **Sign-off checklist:** every item in the scope of work, ticked or explained.
- **Punch list:** anything left open — missing materials, access issues, client decisions — with who owns it next.

## 5. Send it fast

Upload everything the same day. Fast, complete deliverables are what turn a one-off work order into the next ten.

## Quick template

```text
Site: ____  Work order: ____  Date: ____
Arrived: __:__  Left: __:__  Contact: ____
Runs installed: __  Passed: __  Re-tested: __
Photos: before / after / drops / serials
Open items (punch list): ____
```
